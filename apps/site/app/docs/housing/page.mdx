# Housing

Draft. Not counsel-reviewed. This hub is general information for private housing in England and Wales (`EW`). It is not legal advice. Scotland and Northern Ireland are not mixed into these pages. Wales possession has its own official guide, linked from the possession page.

## Instruments named for the evaluation leaves

From the curated-laws seed. Pinpoints only where the gold notes already cite them.

- Possession notices: [Housing Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50/contents) and the [Renters' Rights Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/26/contents). Section 21 transitional provisions **[verify]**.
- Deposits: [Housing Act 2004, Part 6, Chapter 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34/part/6/chapter/4). Award wording **[verify]**.
