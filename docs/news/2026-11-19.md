## Find and restore archived Chats

Archiving a Chat already hid it from your history without deleting it. There is now an **Archived chats** window, opened from the left pane, so you can find those Chats again.

- Search archived Chats by title.
- **Open** an archived Chat to read it. Opening it does not move it back into your history.
- **Unarchive** returns it to your Chat history, with its messages intact.
- There is no delete or purge option here: archive remains a way to tidy up, not to erase.
- The window has clear loading, empty, no-results and error states, and works by keyboard (Escape closes it, focus stays inside while open, arrow keys move between actions).

**Why it matters:** people can put old Chats out of the way without worrying that they have lost them.

Source: [#37](https://github.com/metaspan/chatlegal/pull/37).

## A one-line footer reminder

The sticky footer reminder in the app now reads as a single sentence instead of two stacked lines:

> AI-generated information — check the sources. Not a solicitor. Not legal advice.

On narrow screens it wraps naturally at word boundaries and is never cut off.

**Why it matters:** the limits of the service stay visible on every screen without taking extra space.

Source: [#36](https://github.com/metaspan/chatlegal/pull/36).
