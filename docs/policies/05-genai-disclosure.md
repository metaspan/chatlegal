# 05 · Generative AI disclosure

> **DRAFT · PLAN MODE · for review by qualified counsel · not legal advice.** ChatLegal (chatlegal.uk) · operating entity: [legal entity name · company number]. Version 0.1 · 1 Nov 2026 · Owner: [Policy owner] · Next review: [date].

Supports TNA Q26 (notify GenAI use = Yes), Q25 (not automated legal advice), MoJ principle 8 (Algorithmic transparency). Also SRA/LSA holding-out risk and consumer-law misleading-practice rules (DMCC Act 2024 Part 4).

## Always disclose
| Where | What the user sees |
|---|---|
| Landing page + sign-up | "ChatLegal uses generative AI to explain UK law and point you to official sources. It is not a solicitor and does not give legal advice." |
| Start of every session | Short notice + link to "How ChatLegal works" (methodology page). Must be acknowledged once per account and visible on every session. |
| Every answer | Label "AI-generated information — check the sources" + numbered citations linking to the authoritative source (legislation.gov.uk, GOV.UK, FCL). |
| Escalation | Plain statement why a human is needed and where to go. |
| Exports / info memos | Header: "AI-generated legal information, not legal advice. Generated [date] by ChatLegal." |
| Team/Pro shares | Same labels survive copy/export; cannot be removed by admins. |

## Never
- Present the bot as a person, solicitor, barrister, paralegal or "your lawyer"; persona names (if any) carry the "AI" label.
- Say "advice", "you will win", "your case is strong", or give success percentages.
- Hide that a document or letter template was AI-drafted.

## Methodology page (public)
Plain-English description of: models used (vendor + version family), retrieval sources (allowlist), anonymisation pipeline, triage protocols, eval approach, bias testing, known limits, how to report problems. Updated on material change; version history kept.

## Q25 position
ChatLegal produces general legal information, an options map and escalation — **not** fully automated legal advice, **not** anticipation of legal decisions for a user, and does not decide for the user whether to take action. See 09 for the proposed Q25 answer and the Legal callout to email TNA first.
