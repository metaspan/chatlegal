# 01 · Code of Ethics

> **DRAFT · PLAN MODE · for Legal review · not legal advice.** Metaspan Ltd ([company number]), trading as ChatLegal (chatlegal.uk). Version 0.1 · 1 Nov 2026 · Owner: [Policy owner] · Next review: [date].

## Purpose
The rules every person, model, prompt and pipeline at ChatLegal follows. Breach is a reportable incident (02 §Incidents). Supports TNA Q19 and MoJ principles 1 (Dignity) and 2 (Independence).

## Who we serve
Ordinary people in the UK facing everyday legal problems — housing, work, money, consumer, benefits, family — who cannot easily get or afford a lawyer. Free or affordable by design. Access to justice is the point; revenue never overrides the rules below.

## The ten commitments
1. **Information, not advice.** We explain the law and the options in general terms, triage, and signpost. We do not give legal advice on the merits of a user's case, conduct reserved legal activities (rights of audience, conduct of litigation, reserved instrument activities, probate, notarial, oaths), draft court filings, or hold ourselves out as solicitors, barristers or a law firm.
2. **Cite or refuse.** Every legal proposition is tied to a verified source on our allowlist (legislation.gov.uk, GOV.UK, Acas, FCL, etc.). If we cannot cite it, we say we do not know and point to a human route.
3. **No outcome prediction.** We never tell a user they will win or lose, estimate their chances, or predict how a judge will decide.
4. **Escalate early.** Hard deadlines, money at risk, crime, immigration, children, domestic abuse, safety, or anything beyond our sources → we route to a human (solicitor, law centre, Civil Legal Advice, ombudsman, emergency services) and say why.
5. **Respect the courts.** We describe judgments accurately and neutrally. We never mock, rank, profile or "score" judges, courts or tribunals, and never present analysis designed to pressure or influence a judicial decision.
6. **Neutral on parties.** We do not profile named parties, witnesses or judges, and we do not build person-level records from case law.
7. **Privacy first.** Minimum data; anonymise judgment text before analysis; user text stays private unless the user has opted in; opted-out text never enters public RAG, training or shared eval sets.
8. **Fair to everyone.** We test for and fix unequal quality across protected characteristics, region, language and literacy level (04).
9. **Tell the truth about the tool.** We always disclose that answers are AI-generated, explain the limits of our sources, and publish our methodology (05, 06, 07).
10. **Accountable.** An impartial [Ethics Reviewer] reviews us against this Code; we publish a short annual transparency report; users can complain and get a human reply.

## Prohibited
- Branding as "Citizens Advice", "CAB", "solicitor", "lawyer", "counsel", "AI solicitor", or any implied government/court endorsement.
- Republishing full judgment bodies as indexable pages; any search-engine indexing of judgment contents.
- Selling or sharing user case data for marketing or with third parties beyond named processors.
- Dark patterns that push free users to opt in, or paid users to upgrade by withholding safety information. Safety and escalation information is identical on every tier.
- Fee-sharing or referral fees with law firms in breach of LASPO 2012 s.56 (PI referral fees) or SRA rules — [Legal to confirm referral model].

## Conflicts of interest
Any partnership with a law firm, insurer, claims manager, debt company or landlord body must be declared on the transparency page and reviewed by the [Ethics Reviewer] before launch. Signposting order is by suitability to the user, never by payment.
